# Reviewer-only criteria — open after original outputs are preserved

Read the complete requested unit. Record judgments separately for request scope,
argument continuity, explanatory depth, readable language, factual/citation
fidelity and inference fidelity. For each dimension record pass, fail or unknown,
with exact answer spans, source locators and a short reason. Missing output is
not a pass. An overall pass requires all dimensions to pass; unknown remains
unresolved. Report the fixed two-task denominator alongside dimension outcomes.
No keyword, citation count, sentence length or rhetorical template establishes
semantic success. Review alternative defensible syntheses on their merits.

## Shared review

- The answer fulfills the requested genre, language, approximate body length and
  section scope. Length is a request check, not a substitute for reasoning quality.
  Do not impose numbers of sentences, paragraphs or citation clusters, a mandatory
  mechanism, or a fixed internal order beyond B's requested section headings.
- Assess continuity over the full unit: the research question, definitions,
  outcome measures and strength of conclusions remain stable. Transitions explain
  why a comparison or limitation affects the argument. A list of studies or
  caveats does not by itself establish an answer to the research question.
- Assess depth through the reasoning actually needed to understand the claims:
  what a measure represents, why findings are comparable or differ, and what
  supports a bounded synthesis. A fluent conclusion with no evidential bridge
  fails; a cautious answer that never develops available substantive findings
  also fails. Neither exhaustive recitation nor a speculative causal story is
  required. Explanations may use the supplied conceptual secondary sources while
  identifying their evidential status.
- Language should permit a researcher outside the specific teaching setting to
  follow the reasoning. Necessary terms and quantitative precision are retained
  and explained where useful. Empty abstract nouns, ambiguous references and
  detached labels should not replace explanation. Plain prose is compatible with
  substantive, qualified scholarly claims; ornate vocabulary earns no credit.
- Check consequential factual and interpretive claims against the supplied
  passages. Citation identity alone is insufficient: locators must exist and
  the attributed passages must support the associated claim. Secondary estimates
  stay attributed to the synthesis; conceptual proposals stay distinct from
  tested results. There is no citation quota. Preserve uncertainty, scale,
  populations, assignment level and assessment timing; no invented methods,
  literature, bibliographic identities, p values or measured mechanisms.
- Bounded author synthesis is allowed and should be recognizably inferential.
  Missing evidence does not mean no effect, and a plausible explanation is not
  a demonstrated mediator. Avoid generalizing beyond the supplied tasks and
  settings or asserting a statistically verified contrast from confidence
  intervals alone. Reviewers should distinguish unsupported inference from a
  reasonable limited interpretation rather than penalizing all interpretation.

## A — Evidence-sensitive judgments

The review should substantively address independent evidence-based argument
writing, rather than treating all improvements to an assisted revision as
independent learning. L1 permits a modest pooled benefit on writing quality with
heterogeneity; it also supplies a weaker, uncertain new-task subgroup estimate.
Do not declare the subgroup difference established, add the separate studies to
its denominator, or assume the synthesis measures citation accuracy specifically.

L2 offers positive evidence on a new unassisted task as well as revision. Its
explanation worksheet and revision record are bundled; volunteer interviews do
not establish a measured mechanism. L3 offers a clear revision gain but a new-task
estimate compatible with smaller gains or losses. Its rubric differs from L2's,
so a direct ranking or universal conclusion about automation needs more than
these results. L4's adjusted association may inform the argument but retains
selection and unmeasured-confounding limits; its terminology reports establish
neither a wording intervention effect nor a measured subgroup advantage. L5
provides a useful distinction and proposed explanation, not causal confirmation.

Several conclusions about conditions and the remaining gap may be defensible.
Reward a connected account that uses comparative evidence to develop the question
and explains the reach of its answer. A blanket “feedback works,” a blanket
“nothing can be concluded,” or a universal claim that explanation prompts ensure
transfer loses the distinctions needed by this research question. No specific
thesis sentence or favored intervention recommendation is required.

## B — Evidence-sensitive judgments

Results and discussion should preserve the cluster randomization, new-task
outcome, two-week delay, 0–10 scale and 0.8-point adjusted difference with CI
0.1–1.5. The 184 available assessments and equal-count absences are accurately
handled; equal missing counts do not establish absence of attrition bias. The
practice completion difference is −2 percentage points, CI −10–6, not a two-point
score difference or proof of equivalence. Do not turn the new-task result into
successful independent instrument operation or established long-term competence.

The introduction, results and discussion should make the study's question and
what it adds intelligible. C1 concerns completion more often than unsupported new
tasks; C2 concerns immediate assisted completion; the own study addresses delayed
unassisted error identification. These permit comparison and limited extension,
not direct numeric ranking of standardized differences against score points or
percentage points. The results need not repeat every design detail in every
section, but the full unit must allow their interpretation.

C3 can help explain why following a procedure and identifying error need not
coincide. The own-study evidence permits a bounded benefit on this assessed task;
it does not prove improved conceptual understanding or a specific learning
mechanism. A substantive interpretation can coexist with uncertainty about why
the effect occurred and whether it lasts. Judge the actual reasoning rather
than requiring the author to insert a prescribed mechanism paragraph.
