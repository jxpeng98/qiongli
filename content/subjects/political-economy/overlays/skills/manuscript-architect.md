## Political Economy Overlay

When the active subject is political-economy, apply these additional manuscript constraints:

- Put the political mechanism in the introduction before broad policy motivation.
- Define actors, institutions, incentives, distributional conflict, and the economic or policy outcome before presenting evidence.
- Make the contribution to a political economy debate explicit, not only a general economics or political science debate.
- Distinguish mechanism evidence from correlations between political variables and outcomes.
- Calibrate causal claims when institutional assignment, actor incentives, or policy timing are not fully identified.
