# WorkControl2026 — invented interview report, version 1

This is fictional calibration material, not a published paper. There is no DOI
or private participant data. The following is the complete supplied excerpt.

## WorkControl2026-v1:methods

Twelve analysts at one company volunteered for semi-structured interviews after
trying a new scheduling dashboard for two weeks. The report used thematic analysis.
The interviewer also helped introduce the dashboard. No comparison group, measured
productivity, error-rate data or later followup was collected.

## WorkControl2026-v1:theme

Eight of the twelve interview records described feeling more able to choose the
order of tasks. Four also discussed pressure to respond quickly to visible alerts;
the theme counts can overlap. One illustrative quotation reads: “I can choose
which task to do first, but I keep watching the alert panel.”

## WorkControl2026-v1:limits

The accounts concern perceived control and pressure in this volunteer sample.
They do not estimate prevalence across employees or show a measured productivity
improvement caused by the dashboard. Workload records and the long-term experience
of non-volunteers are unavailable. Participant-level transcripts are not supplied.
