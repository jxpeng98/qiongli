# Scholarly Voice: English and Chinese

Use while drafting Stage F prose and with J2 for stiff, translated or generic
scholarly prose. In revisions, resolve the specific expression problem; in new
drafts, establish clear information order from the start. Use readable, concise
language throughout the manuscript. Preserve evidence and logical meaning, then
choose the clearest wording appropriate to the reader. An academic register does
not justify obscure vocabulary or a harder-to-follow argument. This reference
does not require starting a separate J2 task.

## Shared editing decisions

- Keep names, numbers, quotations, equations, citations, terms, negation,
  quantifiers, scope and uncertainty. Suspected factual errors need a verification
  note; editing is not permission to correct the underlying research silently.
- Follow a supplied voice sample or venue rule without inventing personality,
  experience or a stronger position. Preserve required AI disclosure.
- Locate the point of each paragraph and the role of each sentence. Keep a
  stable topic and identifiable referents; use a paragraph break for a topic
  change when a supported transition cannot connect it naturally.
- Prefer a familiar word or direct verb when it expresses the same meaning.
  Retain necessary technical terms, define them where needed and use them
  consistently. Do not replace a precise term with a vague everyday synonym,
  or rotate synonyms merely to sound sophisticated.
- Cut repetition and empty framing, while retaining the premises, comparison,
  units and qualifications needed to understand the claim. A useful explanation
  may make a passage longer; disconnected short statements are not a successful
  simplification. Do not impose a sentence-length ceiling or readability score.
- Distinguish a genuine contrast from simple addition, and an observed sequence
  from causation. Retain helpful transitions; do not manufacture a missing premise
  with “therefore” or “因此”. Flag the missing reasoning instead.
- On the final continuous read, identify the actual claim, its reason and the
  link to the following point. If a reader must infer an unspecified actor,
  referent or premise, clarify it from inspected material or expose the gap.
  Polished vocabulary cannot make that unresolved step complete.
- Edit at the requested depth. A language pass preserves section order and
  fixed headings; structural editing needs a request that covers it. For coaching,
  show a few meaningful revisions and explain the decisions the author must make.
- Stop when the text meets the request and the source comparison passes. No
  detector target, banned-word list, required sentence pattern or forced slang.

## Language editing and meaning review

G4 checks tone and claim calibration against available evidence. J2 improves
expression while preserving the settled research meaning. Treat language editing
and meaning review as distinct tasks, even when one agent performs both:

- **Language editor:** read a coherent passage and relevant adjacent context;
  improve information order, idiom and sentence connections. Combine, split or
  reorder sentences within the requested paragraph or section scope. An edit
  may be longer when it restores a needed explanation. Remove a qualifier only
  when its deletion leaves the actual uncertainty, attribution and scope intact.
- **Meaning reviewer:** compare the original and edited versions for quantities,
  units, citations and their claim attachment, terminology, negation, quantifiers,
  population/time scope, causal strength and uncertainty. Report the exact
  changed span and its consequence, with a minimal repair when possible. Leave
  valid language choices intact; do not restart a general stylistic rewrite or
  add caveats merely because the prose is direct. No drift is a valid result.

Preserve uncertainty of meaning, not the number or wording of hedges. Observed
results and performed methods can be stated directly. Keep speculative mechanisms
provisional. A missing premise needs substantive review; neither a fluent bridge
nor an extra "may" supplies it. Necessary research limits remain where readers
need them, including independently read abstracts. Repeated boilerplate can be
removed when the local claim remains accurate. Tool failures, pending checks and
editor questions belong in the work record or a brief separate note, not inserted
as manuscript prose; never hide a material research limitation there.

For a requested cross-Host edit, hand over the selected manuscript version,
relevant context, existing terminology/claim constraints and any supplied voice
sample. Receive an edited candidate and unresolved questions. An optional
Codex → Antigravity → Codex sequence assigns editing to Antigravity and meaning
comparison to Codex; it does not select a model, launch a Host, transmit private
material or require multiple agents. Use the user's configured Host/model and
authorized handoff. A bounded edit can use two passages in chat with no new
project, ledger or mandatory handoff file.

Review the returned candidate against the same original version. If either
version changed meanwhile, compare the current versions before applying it.
For registered projects, preserve claim IDs and evidence links and use the
existing preview/approval/CAS owner to save the reviewed candidate. A language
comparison does not establish source support or close an existing evidence gap.
Stop once the requested language issues and actual meaning drift are resolved;
source/argument changes return to their substantive owner.

## English output

Use established collocations and keep the main subject and verb easy to follow.
Reduce noun chains and stacked modifiers without dropping qualifications. Keep
technical terms consistent instead of cycling through near-synonyms. Active or
passive voice and first person follow meaning and venue, not universal rules.
Preserve statistical “significant” and exact Methods procedures.

Carry a recognizable topic into the next sentence, then introduce the new point.
Clarify “this”, “it” or “they” only when the source establishes the referent.
Use sentence length to fit the idea. When translating, reconstruct English
clause order and articles rather than carrying over the source grammar.

Example (expression only):

> We conducted an examination of the association in 120 firms. The estimate was
> 0.18 (p = .03), but the design does not establish causality [12].

Can become:

> We examined the association in 120 firms. The estimate was 0.18 (p = .03), but
> the design does not establish causality [12].

The numbers, citation and causal limitation stay exact. “The effect proves…”
would change the research claim, regardless of how fluent it sounds.

Example (flow with consequential uncertainty):

> In relation to the difference in scores, the estimate was 2 points (95% CI:
> -1 to 5) [S1]. It is important to note that the interval includes zero.
> Equivalence is not something this analysis establishes.

Can become:

> The estimated score difference was 2 points (95% CI: -1 to 5) [S1]. The
> interval includes zero, and the analysis does not establish equivalence.

A meaning reviewer accepts this direct wording. "There was no difference"
or "The groups were equivalent" would change the conclusion and must be
flagged. Restore the affected conclusion, not the original awkward framing.

## 中文输出

按读者、学科和用途确定语体；简繁体随原稿或用户要求。普通学术说明应清楚平实，
正式论文保持学科所需的严谨程度；不把长句、抽象名词或公文化表达当作严谨。

先理顺段落意思，再调整信息顺序。能用具体动词时，减少“对……进行……”等空架子；
拆开堆叠的修饰语和“的”字链。主语可省略，但换话题时要明确指代。不要为了避免
重复而更换关键术语，也不要把所有长句切成短句。

“因此”须有原文支持的推论，“然而”须有真实转折。“此外”不能弥补话题脱节。
保留必要的连接词，不强制排比、四字短语或段末总结。处理中译英、英译中时，
按目标语言重组语序，不新增理由、例子或结论。

例如：

> 本研究对120家企业中的关联进行了检验，估计值为0.18（p = .03）。然而，
> 该研究设计不能确立因果关系[12]。

可改为：

> 本研究检验了120家企业中的关联，估计值为0.18（p = .03），但该研究设计不能
> 确立因果关系[12]。

“相关”不可润色成“导致”，“尚不能排除”不可简化成“没有”。完成后连贯通读，
再对照原稿核查研究含义、数据、引文和限制条件。

例如，已有样本结果支持正相关，待改的是重复限定和句间衔接：

> 在本样本的120家企业中，结果表明，X与Y或许可能存在正相关，估计值为
> 0.18（p = .03）[S2]。需要谨慎指出的是，该研究设计不能确立因果关系。

可改为：

> 在本样本的120家企业中，X与Y呈正相关，估计值为0.18（p = .03）[S2]。
> 该研究设计不能确立因果关系。

含义复核接受这个直接表述，无须恢复“或许可能”或“需要谨慎指出的是”。
“X提高了Y”改变了因果强度；“企业中普遍存在这一关系”扩大了范围，均需指出。
若原稿另有“这一关联可能反映资源差异，但该解释尚未检验”，其中的机制
不确定性必须保留，不能套用前例删成“这一关联反映资源差异”。

以上为合成教学样例；[S1]、[S2] 是样例标记，不是可用于论文的新引文。
