## Geoeconomics Overlay

When the active subject is geoeconomics, match venues by claim type and evidence:

- Use security venues when the central contribution is strategic competition, coercion, deterrence, or national-security statecraft.
- Use IPE venues when the contribution is about sanctions, trade, finance, supply chain governance, or international institutions.
- Use policy venues when the manuscript mainly evaluates implementation, resilience, or economic security tradeoffs.
- Block venue recommendations that rely on geopolitical relevance without evidence of economic instrument, target response, or mechanism.
- State whether the strongest venue expects theory, causal identification, process tracing, policy relevance, or data transparency.
