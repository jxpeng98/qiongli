## Geoeconomics Overlay

When the active subject is geoeconomics, apply these additional manuscript constraints:

- State the strategic economic statecraft claim before broad geopolitical context.
- Define the instrument, sender, target, intermediaries, market response, and strategic competition logic.
- Separate national-security rhetoric from evidence about sanctions, supply chain exposure, financial statecraft, or industrial policy.
- Explain whether the claim is about coercion, resilience, deterrence, signaling, or strategic substitution.
- Calibrate policy implications when timing, exposure, target response, or countermeasure evidence is incomplete.
