# Earlier draft — inspect its claims against the supplied sources

Online applications are already established. The city report gives 63% and the
assistance trial gives 12 percentage points [S1; S2]. Another average estimate
therefore cannot be a contribution. The survey adds later portal use and the
interviews add mechanisms [S3; S4]. Three months of follow-up provides a stronger
test than a fourteen-day endpoint. This motivates a more advanced design.

We distinguish descriptive rates, predictive groups and causal resources. The
paper cannot claim the third level without interactions and falsification.
Its linked records will therefore test robustness, inspect changing samples and
keep unsuccessful mechanisms in the report. These procedures are rare in the
literature. This architecture defines the contribution, while avoiding any claim
to be the first study of assisted digital access.
