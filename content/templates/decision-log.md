# Research Decision Log Template

Save to: `RESEARCH/[topic]/context/decision_log.md`

Use this log for decisions with downstream academic consequences. Do not use it as a generic activity journal.

| Decision ID | Stage | Status | Decision | Rationale | Alternatives Rejected | Evidence Basis | Revisit Trigger | Downstream Impact | Related Claims |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-001 | A | locked | Narrow the project to ... | Broad framing made the RQ unanswerable with available evidence | Alternative broad framing around ... | `framing/research_question.md`; `gap_analysis.md` | New corpus shows the broader framing is feasible | Changes search strategy and contribution statement | |
| DEC-002 | B | revisit-after-E | Treat construct X using definition Y | This definition best matches the dominant literature stream and measurement availability | Definitions Z and W were less stable / not operationalizable | `literature/literature_map.md`; `notes/...` | Synthesis reveals the rival definition explains more evidence | Affects design variables and interpretation language | |

Recommended status values:

- `locked`
- `tentative`
- `revisit-after-B`
- `revisit-after-E`
- `blocked`

`Related Claims` is optional. Reuse existing project claim IDs, separated by
commas or semicolons; leave it blank when no particular claim is affected. These
links mean `informs`, not supporting evidence. Only a `locked` decision creates a
reviewed link; tentative, blocked and revisit decisions remain proposed. A review
report being complete does not make its recommendations accepted decisions.

For a review or venue decision, put the report file and issue/section locator in
Evidence Basis, and the affected manuscript location in Downstream Impact. Carry
the applicable venue/type/stage and dated official-source basis in that report.
Keep uncertainty and the author's unresolved choices visible. Existing logs do
not need a migration; add this column only when useful, through the usual approved
project edit. Never replace evidence or delete an earlier decision to tidy the graph.
