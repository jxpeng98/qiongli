# Stage M - Dissertation And Major Project

Stage M supports dissertation, thesis, capstone, and major-project planning across degree levels.

Use Stage M when the user needs a dissertation plan, chapter architecture, supervisor feedback integration, milestone risk tracking, final readiness review, or viva/defense preparation.

Required boundaries:

- Calibrate contribution expectations to degree level.
- Do not imply supervisor approval.
- Do not invent ethics approval, data access, fieldwork, interviews, supervisor comments, or handbook rules.
- Reuse existing A-K research tasks for literature, methods, writing, proofread, and presentation work rather than duplicating them.

## Keep chapters and decisions coherent

Use the actual handbook and degree level to distinguish a monograph, article-based
thesis or practice/major project. Map each chapter to its question, contribution,
evidence and dependencies in `dissertation/chapter_map.md`. Multiple papers need
an explicit connecting argument and honest treatment of overlapping data/text;
the introduction/conclusion cannot claim a synthesis the chapters do not support.

Record supervisor comments with date, source/locator, requested action, status
and changed location. Reconcile conflicting advice as a decision to resolve,
not invented approval. Milestones depend on data access, review/ethics, analysis
and feedback lead time; preserve feasible alternatives when a dependency fails.

Before final readiness, check cross-chapter definitions, samples, numbers,
citations, reused figures, contribution claims and required permissions against
the same version. Defense preparation should explain the original contribution,
method choice, weakest assumption, alternatives and limits using actual evidence.
Do not predict examination outcomes or substitute a polished deck for unfinished
research. Keep outstanding chapter/evidence requirements visible in the handoff.
