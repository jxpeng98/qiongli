## Review procedure and shared checks

Review the entire actual final answer, including tables and handoff. For every
substantive claim, retain an exact answer span and link it to a supplied locator,
or mark it unsupported/needs evidence. A reviewer summary is not source evidence.
Methodological recommendations may go beyond the source if explicitly conditional;
they must not be represented as the authors' procedure or an executed action.
Use `pass`, `fail`, or `unreviewed` with reasons and implicated spans for each
check. A checkpoint passes only when all applicable checks pass. Never repair a
captured answer during review. Same-agent role changes count as self-review.

## Q1 — quantitative education

Use `Ritchie2013Retrieval:participants`, `allocation`, `allocation-procedure`,
`mind-map-allocation`, `exposure`, `testing`, `denominator`, `analysis`,
`adjusted-findings` and `mechanism-limit`.

- 109 children were recruited, aged 8–12, from four Primary 5/7 classes at one
  Edinburgh school. One learning sheet was lost, leaving 108 sheets; do not
  silently label 109 as every analysis denominator, or infer a new participant
  exclusion from this missing sheet. The reported F degrees of freedom remain
  those reported; any reconciliation not supported here is an open question.
- Retrieval assignment occurred within classes and the stated procedure was
  alternation through the register (authors call this random). Mind mapping was
  assigned between classes. Do not describe both factors as independently
  individually randomized, or invent allocation concealment/cluster adjustment.
  A proposed trial should consider this distinction without claiming an ICC,
  design effect, multilevel estimate or adequate power has already been obtained.
- The outcome was a factual recall quiz four days later, scored blind to
  condition. Percentages are descriptive; z-scores were used for analysis.
  Reported analysis is a 2×2 ANCOVA with facts recorded during learning as the
  selected covariate. This is not necessarily an unaffected pretreatment measure:
  the supplied procedure places note-taking inside the learning intervention.
- Retrieval F(1,104)=6.33, p=.01, partial eta-squared=.06 is an adjusted result.
  Mind mapping has no reported main effect (p=.17), but the reported interaction
  has p=.001. No requirement to repeat every statistic; any included value must
  be correct. Do not convert eta-squared into a percent score gain or invent a CI.
- The causal mechanism is not fully separated from feedback/restudy. Limit
  implementation advice to this outcome/context. R1 lacks the subsequent
  sensitivity paragraph and supplement: no invented unadjusted result or claim
  of demonstrated robustness. Do not require the runner to discover withheld
  facts. Reasoned provisional confidence is acceptable; an unqualified universal
  implementation recommendation is not.

## L1 — qualitative education

- 19 interviewees: nine men, ten women; 15 STEM/four non-STEM majors from a single
  private religious university. Instructors nominated performance/participation
  categories; 43 women and 25 men were invited, 10/9 participated. Do not call
  recruitment random, a representative survey, or convert mention counts into
  population prevalence/significance.
- Semi-structured interviews varied by participant, lasting 40–50 minutes. Most
  were online, eight women in person. Account for researcher positionality,
  shared institutional/religious context and possible disclosure/mode differences
  as interpretive limits, without claiming measured causal confounding.
- Analysis was iterative inductive thematic analysis drawing on thematic
  networks: initial ideas, coding framework, independent paired line coding,
  consensus discussion and theme refinement. The Expectancy Value Theory framing
  came after broad themes. Do not turn this into an a priori deductive EVT test,
  claim a new reanalysis, or invent a kappa/coefficient.
- Saturation was claimed by the authors; supplied transcripts do not allow the
  reviewer to independently establish it. Participants did not comment on
  transcripts/findings. Never award member checking simply because other
  credibility practices are present.
- Three themes concern external factors, individual weighing, and cautious
  gender-related patterns. The authors did not aim to statistically compare men
  and women; performance mix and context limit interpretation/transferability.
  The supplied Julia quote is an individual account, not an intervention effect.
  A translation must remain faithful and labeled. Recommendations are proposed
  interview design or pilot questions, not validated effects on participation.
