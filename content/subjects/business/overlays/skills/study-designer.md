## Business Overlay

- State whether the business study is theory-building, theory-testing, mixed-method, qualitative, quantitative, review, or methods work.
- Define the unit of analysis before choosing data, constructs, or model structure.
- Tie sampling logic to theory, not only data access or convenience.
- For qualitative designs, document case selection, interview protocol, coding logic, triangulation, and negative cases.
- For quantitative designs, document construct validity, source data, level mismatch, endogeneity, and robustness checks.
- Use doctoral-level journal contribution as the quality bar even when the user is an undergraduate or master's researcher.
