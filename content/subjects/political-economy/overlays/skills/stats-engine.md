## Political Economy Overlay

When the active subject is political-economy, apply these additional statistical constraints:

- Cluster or aggregate inference at the political, institutional, or policy assignment level.
- Test whether treatment timing is endogenous to political incentives, distributional conflict, or institutional capacity.
- Report pre-trends, anticipation, and policy timing checks when designs rely on reform or event timing.
- Include alternative political mechanism checks before upgrading a policy effect into a causal political economy claim.
- Treat institutional scope, regime type, and state capacity as boundary conditions rather than generic controls.
