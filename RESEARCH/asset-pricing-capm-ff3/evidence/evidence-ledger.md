# Evidence Ledger Review

## Inventory

- Seven central claims are recorded in `claim-evidence-ledger.csv`.
- Five claims point to generated analysis outputs, one to official dataset documentation, and one to a primary methods paper note.
- Every row has a portable source ID, project-relative artifact path, explicit limitation, confidence, and supported status.

## Review Result

The central quantitative claims are directly traceable to deterministic outputs. Interpretation is deliberately bounded: the ledger contains no causal, predictive, investment, joint-model, or universal-validity claim. The residual-diagnostic and shared-construction limitations are first-class claims rather than footnotes.
