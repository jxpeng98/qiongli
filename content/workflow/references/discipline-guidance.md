# Discipline and method guidance

Use when the research setting changes search terms, evidence, measurement,
analysis or reporting. Start with the requested deliverable and read one relevant
guide below. For genuinely mixed work, add only the section needed from a second
guide. A quick language edit or supplied-paper explanation needs no subject tour.

## Choose a lens

Profile names below are existing IDs, not new commands. If the selected package
contains the matching `skills/domain-profiles/[domain].yaml`, use its applicable
method diagnostics. Focused packages may omit other profiles; use the available
guide and supplied protocol without pretending to load an absent resource.

These guides extend the shared research library. They do not register new subject
packages, change a project's locked subject, install a database/library, select a
model or authorize data access. Preserve project-local choices and explain a
material mismatch before proposing an update through the existing owner.

## Select methods by the claim

| Intended claim | Decision to settle | Existing owner |
|---|---|---|
| Describe a population or pattern | Sampling frame, denominator, weights, uncertainty and coverage | Stage C; `skills/I_code/stats-engine.md` |
| Estimate an effect | Target population, intervention/exposure, comparator, time, estimand and identification assumptions | `references/stage-C-design.md` |
| Predict or classify | Intended use, independent evaluation unit, leakage-safe split, calibration and transportability | Computing or health guide; Stage I |
| Interpret experience, process or text | Analytic tradition, corpus/case boundary, researcher position and source-to-interpretation trail | `skills/E_synthesis/qualitative-coding.md`; relevant discipline guide |
| Integrate qualitative and quantitative evidence | Why both are needed, sequence/priority, sampling relationship and integration point | Education/psychology guide; Stage C/E/F |
| Synthesize a literature | Review type, eligible evidence, unit of synthesis, appraisal and whether pooling is defensible | `references/stage-B-literature.md`, `references/stage-E-synthesis.md` |
| Establish a theorem or conceptual argument | Definitions, assumptions, derivation, counterexamples and scope | `references/stage-A-framing.md`, `references/stage-F-writing.md` |

Do not rank qualitative, descriptive, causal and predictive studies on one ladder.
Legacy profile fields such as `standard_hierarchy`, numerical heuristics and
`required_tables` are contextual starting points, not universal pass criteria.
An agreed protocol or applicable formal standard remains binding. Apply the
specific method contract and explain why a diagnostic is required or inapplicable.

## Carry useful information forward

At a formal handoff, reuse `references/stage-handoff-contract.md` to preserve the
research question/claim type, population or corpus, unit, measurement/source
definitions, selected method and its unresolved assumptions. Keep source versions,
access limits and exploratory amendments attached to the same claims.
Use the existing stage artifacts; do not create a parallel discipline dossier.

Source links in each guide are starting points for the named method or standard,
checked on 2026-10-01. They are not evidence that a user's study complies. Verify
the applicable edition, extension, venue and jurisdiction when a current rule
affects the deliverable; unavailable verification remains an explicit gap.
