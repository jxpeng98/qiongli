## Geoeconomics Overlay

When the active subject is geoeconomics, apply these additional design constraints:

- Specify the sender-target-instrument-response chain before selecting a method.
- Distinguish announcement, implementation, enforcement, and response timing for sanctions or export-control designs.
- Construct exposure using pre-treatment trade, firm, supply chain, financial, or sector data.
- Check strategic competition confounding, policy bundling, evasion, substitution, and countermeasures.
- For case designs, document process evidence for statecraft intent, target response, and market or firm adaptation.
