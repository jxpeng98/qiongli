## Geoeconomics Overlay

When the active subject is geoeconomics, map the literature by economic statecraft instrument and strategic response:

- Classify each stream by instrument: sanctions, export controls, investment screening, industrial policy, financial statecraft, or supply chain policy.
- Identify sender, target, intermediaries, strategic objective, affected market, and response channel.
- Separate coercion, deterrence, resilience, signaling, and strategic competition claims.
- Track whether evidence shows policy rhetoric, economic exposure, target response, or actual strategic effect.
- Flag gaps where supply chain or sanctions claims ignore evasion, substitution, or countermeasures.
