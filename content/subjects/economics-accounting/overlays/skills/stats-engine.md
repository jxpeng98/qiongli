## Quality Bar

- The estimand, identifying variation, and accounting construct must be stated before estimator choice.
- Panel, event-study, DID, IV, or RD models must align treatment timing with fiscal-year reporting windows.
- Standard errors must be justified for firm, industry, auditor, market, or state-level dependence; cluster choice must match assignment and reporting channels.
- Archival accounting designs must report sample filters, winsorization, missingness handling, and alternative proxy checks.
- Robustness must include at least one construct-validity check and one identification-threat check when causal language is used.

## Common Pitfalls

- Treating an accounting proxy as the theoretical construct without measurement-risk discussion.
- Using naive TWFE under staggered adoption without cohort or event-time diagnostics.
- Mixing fiscal-year and calendar-year timing without documenting alignment rules.
- Clustering at the firm level when shocks, disclosure mandates, or auditor effects operate at a broader level.
- Interpreting archival accounting correlations as mechanisms without ruling out reporting incentives, governance, financing, or selection channels.
