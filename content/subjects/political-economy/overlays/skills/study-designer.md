## Political Economy Overlay

When the active subject is political-economy, apply these additional design constraints:

- Specify the actor-institution-outcome chain before selecting a method.
- Define the distributional conflict and explain why actors have incentives to pursue the observed policy or economic outcome.
- Align the unit of analysis with the political mechanism: voter, elite, firm, locality, agency, party, state, or international actor.
- For quantitative designs, document policy timing, institutional clustering, endogenous adoption, and alternative political mechanisms.
- For qualitative or historical designs, document case selection, process evidence, rival explanations, and institutional boundary conditions.
