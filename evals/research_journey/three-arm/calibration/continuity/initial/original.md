## 中文

在一家公司的12名自愿受访分析员中，8份访谈记录提到，试用排程看板两周后，受访者感觉更能自主安排任务顺序。这些叙述反映的是该样本的主观体验，不能证明看板提高了实际生产率。访谈也涉及提醒带来的响应压力，两类体验可能并存。缺少对照组、工作量记录与后续追踪，限制了对因果关系及长期影响的判断。〔WorkControl2026-v1:methods; theme; limits〕

## English

In interviews with 12 volunteer analysts at one company, eight interview records
described a greater sense of control over task order after two weeks of trying a
scheduling dashboard. These accounts reflect perceived experience in this sample;
they do not establish an improvement in measured productivity. Pressure to respond
to alerts was also discussed and could coexist with perceived control. The lack
of a comparison group, workload records and later followup limits conclusions
about causality and longer-term effects.
