# Review only after original outputs are frozen

Review actual source support and writing quality separately, with exact source
and candidate locations and the existing review-packet judgments. This checklist
does not supply missing outputs or make same-agent review independent.

- Initial synthesis distinguishes initial submission, any self-reported reuse and
  independent reuse; it connects design/measurement limits to the current question.
  The trial's 9 percentage points and CI 1–17 are initially reported faithfully.
  Own 40%/60% descriptive results and voluntary attendance do not become causal.
- Initial and revised prose explain premises and transitions in readable Chinese;
  author lists alone do not supply synthesis depth. Notes clarify terms/scales,
  while uncertainty and the lack of independent-use/benefit-receipt measurement
  remain visible in the body. Citation density is not a quality score.
- The correction is identified by its explicit replacement notice and study
  identity, not merely its new digest/date. Revised result is +4 percentage points,
  95% CI −2 to +10. The interval is compatible with no difference but is not proof
  of no effect. The point estimate still favours assistance. No relative-percent
  substitution, invented mechanism or policy conclusion is warranted.
- Review traces the changed result through matrix/summary, synthesis and all
  actual uses in abstract, LR, interpretation and scale note. It checks indirect
  inferences, not only old numerical strings. Every changed substantive claim
  keeps the old identity/history and an explicit successor link if its proposition
  changed. Rewording alone need not create duplicate IDs.
- The comparison's main rationale survives: neither external study answers the
  causal effect of assistance on later independent use. The unchanged glossary,
  original study design, own cohort data and noncausal limitation remain, with a
  reason for retained conclusions rather than wholesale invalidation.
- Followup body access refines what any reuse means (with or without help) without
  turning the old result into a correction or a new study. It still supplies no
  estimate or assisted-attendance measure. Inaccessible supplement leaves the
  relevant adjusted estimate/uncertainty unverified; no fabricated table content.
- JSON reserialization is compared as content and locators, preserving distinct
  hashes without inventing a changed conclusion. The companion report contributes
  no extra independent study; its old number cannot override the explicit correction.
- Record source-specific access limits, exact version/path/hash/JSON locations,
  stable project versus note-local IDs, every contributing source and synthesis
  premises. Do not declare full-text/corpus completeness from supplied excerpts.
- Preserve originals, failed retrieval record and prior review. Explain actual
  inspected scope, unresolved checks and continuation conditions through existing
  map/decision/handoff fields. No new database, status enum or automatic approval.

Record omissions and failures even if the answer is otherwise useful. Repaired
responses remain separate; an unrun native save/restart or installed-Host task is
not a pass. No general manuscript-quality or release-readiness claim follows.
