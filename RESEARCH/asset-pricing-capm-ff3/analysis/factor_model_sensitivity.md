## Interpretation Boundary

The aggregate comparison is not a universal dominance result. Weighting changes portfolio returns, and portfolio-level exceptions show that FF3 does not reduce every intercept. The result is limited to the official 25-portfolio grid, monthly frequency, 1963-07 through 2026-06, and the pinned archive bytes.
