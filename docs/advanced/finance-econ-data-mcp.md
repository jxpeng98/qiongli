# Finance and economics data

Qiongli's native literature tools discover research papers. They do not provide a
built-in market-data, filings or economic-series API. The earlier proposed
`qiongli_finance_data_status` and `qiongli_finance_data_search` names are not
installed Qiongli 2 tools.

For a study that needs data, identify the required series, filings, date range,
version and access terms. Use a suitable tool actually available in the Host, or
supply authorized files. Preserve the source, retrieval date, transformations and
method limits in the research record. Search results about a dataset are not the
dataset itself.

Domain profiles can help choose relevant methods and reporting checks without
creating another data runtime. Working-paper metadata, published-version links
and access to full text are separate questions. See [subject guidance](subject-packaging-model.md)
and [rigorous literature search](rigorous-literature-search.md).
