## R1 — First-session training trial

Eight workshops were randomly assigned to use an interactive maintenance guide
or static instructions. There were 160 trainees in total. The outcome was
completion of the first practice task within forty minutes. The workshop-adjusted
difference was 8 percentage points in favour of the interactive guide, with a
95% confidence interval of 2 to 14 percentage points. The study did not measure
conceptual understanding, later performance or completion without assistance.
The excerpt does not describe the trainees' prior training or recruitment.

## R2 — Later-use survey

A separate observational survey followed 96 participants from another training
programme for eight weeks. Higher self-reported confidence was associated with
more self-reported reuse of a maintenance procedure after adjustment for prior
experience. No numerical association or uncertainty interval is supplied. Use
of the interactive guide was not recorded. The survey did not measure successful
independent completion. The excerpt gives no information about attrition.
