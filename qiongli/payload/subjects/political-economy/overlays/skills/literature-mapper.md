## Political Economy Overlay

When the active subject is political-economy, map the literature by political mechanism rather than by topic label alone:

- Identify the institution, actor set, incentive structure, distributional conflict, and policy or economic outcome for each stream.
- Separate electoral, bureaucratic, coalition, state-capacity, and interest-group mechanisms.
- Track whether each study supports a causal claim, interpretive mechanism, or descriptive association.
- Record boundary conditions such as regime type, administrative capacity, policy domain, and development level.
- Flag gaps where the literature names an institution but does not show how it changes incentives or policy outcomes.
