# Captured academic-quality evidence

- Eligibility criteria: population, intervention, comparator, outcomes, study design, dates, and languages are explicit.
- PRISMA screening counts: identified, deduplicated, screened, excluded, retrieved, and included totals reconcile.
- Risk of bias: each included study has a domain-level judgment and rationale.
- Strength and heterogeneity of evidence: certainty, inconsistency, and limits on pooling are distinguished.
