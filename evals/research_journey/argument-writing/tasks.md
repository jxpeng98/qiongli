# Two independent writing requests

Use only the excerpts and study record in `sources.md`. All materials are
invented evaluation inputs, not real publications. Treat A and B as independent
requests, each with its own complete answer. Local references such as
[L2, p. 8, ¶2] and [C1, p. 3, ¶1] identify supplied passages. Cite consequential
source-based claims with these locators; cite the supplied own-study record as
[Study, §S1] etc. No external search, project creation, tool execution or research
data writes are requested. Deliver the requested prose, without an internal
checklist, claim ledger, outline, recommendations to the user or commentary on
how you wrote it. Do not invent bibliographic metadata.

## A — Complete English literature review

Write a complete literature review of approximately 700–1,000 body words for a
paper asking: **Under what conditions does formative feedback help novice
university students improve their independent evidence-based argument writing?**
The audience is education researchers who know basic quantitative methods but
are unfamiliar with the individual studies. Use L1–L5. Develop an argument that
answers the question as far as the supplied evidence permits, compares relevant
findings and identifies the remaining research gap. Explain important concepts
and evidence relationships rather than producing an annotated bibliography.
You may draw your own bounded synthesis, clearly distinguishing it from reported
findings. Give the review a suitable title; no abstract or reference list is
needed. The word range refers to whitespace-separated body words, excluding the
title and citation brackets. Paragraph organization is your choice.

## B — 中文引言、结果与讨论

请仅根据C1—C3和Study写一篇论文的“引言”“结果”“讨论”三部分，共约900—1,300字。
研究问题是：**在本科实验课中，要求学生先解释操作理由，再查阅提示，是否有助于其
在新任务中独立识别测量误差？** 读者了解基本研究方法，但不熟悉本实验课。
引言应交代值得研究的问题及已有证据，结果应准确报告本研究，讨论应解释结果对
研究问题意味着什么，并与已有材料形成实质比较。可作有依据的作者综合，但需让
读者分辨观察结果、解释与尚未检验的推断。只交付这三个标题及正文，不需摘要、
参考文献表或后续研究方案。正文非空白Unicode字符（含标点、数字）计入字数，
标题和方括号引用不计入；无需在答案中报告字数。
