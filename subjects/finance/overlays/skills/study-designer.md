## Finance Overlay

- State the finance claim type before specifying variables, returns, factors, or estimators.
- Define the asset universe, sample window, return frequency, event timing, and benchmark model.
- Audit look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, delisting returns, stale prices, event-date leakage, and overlapping observations.
- For corporate finance designs, separate causal identification from risk-adjusted association.
- For asset pricing designs, predefine factor models, portfolio sorts, Fama-MacBeth steps, and standard-error corrections.
- Use doctoral-level journal contribution as the quality bar even when the user is an undergraduate or master's researcher.
