# Reading calibration checks — not a generation prompt

Provenance: newly authored fictional calibration, excluded from main effect
estimates. These are source-specific review aids, not real scientific conclusions.

Necessary content for common coverage:

1. P1 immediate observed means 15.0 vs 13.5: 1.5 **raw points on a 20-point
   scale**, not 1.5% or individual change. Its reported 95% interval −0.3 to 3.3
   includes zero; the result is uncertain, not proof of benefit or exact no effect.
2. P1 randomized 24 per arm but tested 22 per arm. Distinguish randomized and
   observed denominators. No supplied intention-to-treat result or baseline gain.
3. P1's immediate test allowed materials. P1-F uses the same study, no materials,
   four weeks later, with 16/14 returning and means 12.1/12.8. It is not a third
   independent replication. The −0.7 gap has no supplied uncertainty estimate.
4. P2 is independent: advanced students at another institution, one-week novel
   transfer without materials, 40/42 observed, 18.1/18.3 on a 30-point scale;
   −0.2 points with interval −1.0 to 0.6. Do not pool its raw difference with P1.
5. Explain why outcomes, populations, access to materials, timing and followup
   attrition constrain comparison. At least the immediate/transfer/retention
   distinction and same-study followup relationship must appear in synthesis,
   not solely in a disconnected list of citations.
6. Conclusion stays within these supplied fictional reports: no established
   general durable benefit, no universal ineffectiveness, no claim of learning
   decline from subtracting means of different observed sets/tests.
7. Figure 1 is unavailable; actual reading covers supplied text/tables only.
   No invented shape, outlier, p-value, effect size or unseen full-text claim.

Check all added claims, even outside this checklist. The task permits necessary
details in either prose or the comparison table; do not require every number in
both. Necessary relationships must be expressed coherently in the prose. Missing
optional details do not automatically invalidate reasoning.

Use report/section/table locators. Do not require Qiongli syntax or claim IDs.
Keep the 260–360 non-whitespace-character prose count separate from source support;
the comparison table and standalone source markers are excluded as specified in
the common task. Record actual counting rules and any ambiguity, not an invented
length pass. Expression review needs actual blinded reader feedback.
