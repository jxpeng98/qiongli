# Review criteria — do not supply as the writing prompt

Review complete answers against `sources.md` after preserving the original
answers. Retain each attempted task, missing answer and failure. Give a concrete
answer span and source basis for each judgment; a heading, citation count or
model self-description is not proof of quality. No historical baseline score is
changed. These synthetic cases do not represent the user's unavailable manuscript.

| Dimension | Inspect in the actual answer |
| --- | --- |
| Flow | Clear subjects/referents, idiomatic clause order, connected reasoning; no empty abstract phrases or invented causal transitions. Preserve the requested register and technical distinctions. |
| LR depth and relevance | Case A compares what the evidence can establish about initial submission and later independent use. Explain consequential differences in design/outcome/access, and tie the synthesis to the proposed linked records. An author list or a generic “relevant to this study” does not meet this criterion. Do not require every detail of every source. |
| Support coverage | Source-dependent background as well as findings have matching nearby citations. S1 is one city's submitted applications, not global older residents or independent use. More citations alone cannot establish support; original plans must remain planned. |
| Source fidelity | S2 is centre-randomized, age 65+, 480 applicants, a 12 percentage-point initial-submission difference, CI 2–22; it does not establish later autonomy. Do not invent its recruitment/training. S3 is a different, observational, self-report outcome and does not contradict S2 merely by direction. S4's participant accounts are not measured prevalence or established mechanisms. |
| Notes | Case B has no notes. In C preserve the existing context note, use a distinct useful explanatory note if provided, cite its source, and leave consequential limitations in the body. Markers and definitions must resolve without duplicate definitions. Do not fabricate locators or hide unsupported claims in notes. |
| Edit fidelity and scope | B preserves 120 firms, 0.18, p = .03, association, causal limitation and [S5], without inventing the construct or research details. No unsolicited project, bibliography, search or checklist. C removes/narrows unsupported global, autonomy and priority claims. |

Language/depth judgments are qualitative review, not automatic factual scores.
Report structural/projection checks separately from answer review. A materialized
guidance/subagent trial is not an installed-Host observation or evidence of
cross-model, full-manuscript or expert acceptance. Cases used for corrections
become regressions; a replay is not a fresh held-out cohort or a controlled
before/after improvement result.
