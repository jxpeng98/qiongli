## PromptLab2026:identity

Report P1, version 1. One study of 48 first-year students at one institution.
Individual random assignment allocated 24 students to worked-example prompts and
24 to ordinary practice. Two students per arm missed the immediate test. The
reported analysis includes 22 per arm; no intention-to-treat analysis is supplied.
Students could consult their assigned materials during the immediate 20-point
test. The abstract says the prompt arm scored higher on the immediate test.

includes zero; the report does not establish the exact absence of an effect.

transfer test was completed without materials by 40 prompt and 42 practice
students. The test has a 0–30 scale and differs from P1's immediate test.
