## Business Overlay

When the active subject is business, apply these additional manuscript constraints:

- Frame the paper around a doctoral-level journal contribution to a management, strategy, organization, marketing, or operations conversation.
- Separate the business phenomenon, theory mechanism, empirical setting, and managerial implication.
- For qualitative work, show the evidence chain from field material to constructs, not only representative quotes.
- For quantitative work, connect each hypothesis to constructs, level of analysis, sample construction, and robustness evidence.
- Treat undergraduate-and-above use as acceptable only when the output is raised to publishable journal standards.
