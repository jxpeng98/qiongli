### Results

The implementation of the interactive guide occasioned an enhancement in the
realisation of first-task completion. The difference was 8 percentage points,
with a 95% confidence interval of 2 to 14 percentage points. This pertains to
completion within forty minutes across the eight randomised workshops and their
160 trainees. Independent completion was not measured. [R1]

### Discussion

The interpretative salience of this result resides in the distinction between
an initial completion advantage and a subsequent independent-performance claim.
The latter does not follow from the former. A separate eight-week survey links
confidence to reported procedure reuse, adjusting for prior experience, but does
not measure guide use or successful independent completion. [R2] The inferential
configuration thus preserves a boundary on the portability of the initial
finding. Resolving this requires information about later independent performance.
