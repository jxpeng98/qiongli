## Extracted Point

Sharpe develops an equilibrium account in which the relevant risk of an individual asset is its nondiversifiable responsiveness to common economic activity and expected return is linear in that exposure. The empirical project uses the market excess-return regression as a benchmark operationalization; it does not test all equilibrium assumptions.

## Limitation For This Project

The theory's restrictive borrowing/lending and homogeneous-expectations assumptions are not evaluated by portfolio time-series fit.
